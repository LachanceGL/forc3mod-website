What it does

Everything but the airbrush.

FORC3 Designer is built for people who want a great-looking livery without learning Blender or Substance Painter. Start from a template or a blank shell, paint in layers, and export straight into your game folder.

  • ✓ Real-time 3D preview on the actual car model
  • ✓ Layer-based painting with blend modes and masks
  • ✓ Sponsor decal, number and livery template library
  • ✓ One-click export straight into your AC EVO skins folder

How it works

From blank shell to grid-ready.

Three steps, all in the browser.

🚗

1. Pick a car

Choose from FORC3MOD's growing library of UV-mapped car templates.

🖌️

2. Paint & layer

Build your livery in layers — base coat, sponsors, numbers, weathering.

📦

3. Export & race

Download a game-ready skin package, or push it straight to your AC EVO install.
