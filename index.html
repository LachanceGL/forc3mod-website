Everything you need to know before dropping a FORC3MOD pack into your sim.

Still stuck? Ask us
Do your mods work with the latest Assetto Corsa EVO build?

Yes. Every release is tested against the current public EVO build, and we patch fast whenever the game updates.

How do I install a FORC3MOD pack?

Each pack ships with a one-click installer and a written guide. Drop it in, launch the game, and it shows up in your content list.

Are the mods free or paid?

We offer a large free library plus premium packs. Buying a premium mod includes free lifetime updates.

Can I request a custom car, track or livery?

Absolutely — that's what the "Get a Mod" button is for. Tell us what you want and we'll scope it out.

Will mods get me banned or break online play?

Our content is single-player and private-lobby friendly. Always follow server rules for any ranked or official online play.

that actually feels real?

Request a custom mod or join the community. We'll get back to you within 48 hours.
