Still stuck? Ask us
Is FORC3 Designer free?

Yes — FORC3 Designer is 100% free to use, no catches.

Is this an official Assetto Corsa EVO tool?

No. FORC3 Designer is unofficial and not affiliated with Kunos Simulazioni or the Assetto Corsa EVO team.

What platforms does it support?

Windows only, for now.

Do I need design experience to use it?

No — it's built with a fast, user-friendly interface so anyone can create a livery, no 3D or design background required.

When can I download it?

We're finalizing the public download. Head to the FORC3 Designer page and sign up to be notified the moment it's live.
